## Forge

Forge is a deterministic replay and task-execution engine I have been building
since July 2026, using Python, C++20 and SQLite. It is a local-first execution
engine with durable coordination and reproducible results.

## Where it stands

I am currently implementing the deterministic single-process reference. Once
that is in place, the next step is adding multi-process scheduling.
